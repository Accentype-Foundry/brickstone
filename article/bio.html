Bio

Miklós Ferencz (pronounced /mik-losh fe-rentz/) is a type designer, graphic and web designer, and developer working at the intersection of art and technology. Raised and educated in Budapest, Hungary, he completed a postgraduate degree in type design at the Atelier National de Recherche Typographique (ANRT) in Nancy, France (2015), and has been based in Marseille for the last few years. As the founder of Accentype foundry, he focuses on building typefaces and engineering them to perform effortlessly across digital and print media. Alongside running his foundry, Miklós works on visual identities, editorial layouts, book designs, and web development, while volunteering his creative and digital expertise to NGOs to help drive lasting social impact.
